Answer a missing value with one tap, not an open text field

The free-text field was always on screen, which made the capture sheet look
like a form again -- the exact thing the conversation engine exists to remove.
The common answers are now chips, and "Other..." folds the field open only when
the chips do not cover what is wanted.

    Sure. What date?
    [Tomorrow] [Tonight] [Tomorrow morning] [This weekend] [Next week] [Other...]

A chip and a typed phrase go through one handler, so the two can never save
differently. The mic still sits beside the field when it is opened.

Verified: conversation 16 checks, imagechoice 10, ui 140, plus plan, autosave,
voice and deadcode.
