Put the answer in the sentence, not beside it

Tapping "Tomorrow" put the answer in a separate card with a transcript, while the
box still read "remind me" -- two panels competing for one small sheet.
The answer now becomes words in the capture box itself:

    remind me  ->  remind me tomorrow  ->  remind me tomorrow 10 AM

and the next question drops in flush underneath, with no card border, no fill
and no side padding. The box is the only record; the separate transcript is
gone. A refusal is now one line in the hint line rather than a panel of its own.

Two details that would have broken it: appending does not re-run the reader (that
would read the new sentence as fresh, throw the conversation away and re-open the
question just answered), and the extended text is re-registered with the auto-create,
which compares against the text the reading was made from. Tapping the same chip
twice does not write "tomorrow tomorrow".

Verified: 8/8 suites -- ui 141, conversation 19, imagechoice 10, plan, autosave,
voice, deadcode, gaps.
